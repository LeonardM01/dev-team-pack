# Copywriting Blunders - Audit Checklist

Used in Phase 3 (Audit & Rewrite). Check every page section against all ten. Report findings by ID (e.g. "Hero: B4, B7"). Each entry: how to detect it, how to fix it.

## B1 - We-centric instead of you-centric
**Detect:** Sentences leading with "We", "Our platform", "Our award-winning…".
**Fix:** Rewrite around the buyer's experience: "You will save…", "Your team can…".

## B2 - Features confused with benefits
**Detect:** Technical specs with no translation ("10,000 RPM motor").
**Fix:** Run it through FAB: feature → advantage → life benefit ("Blends your morning smoothie in 4 seconds flat").

## B3 - Awareness misalignment
**Detect:** Feature-heavy technical copy aimed at cold traffic that doesn't yet know it has the problem.
**Fix:** Match the framework to the confirmed awareness level (see frameworks.md matrix); educate before you pitch.

## B4 - Vague corporate fluff
**Detect:** "Streamline workflows", "maximize synergy", "all-in-one ecosystem" - buzzwords that force the reader to guess what you sell.
**Fix:** Replace with concrete, specific language - ideally exact VoC phrases from the research brief.

## B5 - CTA written last
**Detect:** Scattered messaging with no single conversion goal; multiple competing CTAs.
**Fix:** Anchor every section to the one confirmed conversion goal; every block builds a slippery slope toward that action.

## B6 - Blind, unbacked claims
**Detect:** "Double your revenue", "change your life" with no proof adjacent.
**Fix:** Attach a case study, testimonial, or concrete metric immediately next to the claim - or delete the claim.

## B7 - Cryptic hero slogan
**Detect:** Artsy or poetic tagline at the top that doesn't communicate what's offered within 3 seconds.
**Fix:** Hero must state what it is, who it's for, and the core benefit - cleverness comes second.

## B8 - Walls of text
**Detect:** Dense paragraphs, no headers, no bullets, uniform line lengths.
**Fix:** Break into snackable bits: bold headers, bullets, varied line lengths, white space. Visitors are professional skimmers.

## B9 - Avoided objections
**Detect:** No mention of price concerns, contracts, implementation time, security, or switching costs.
**Fix:** Name the objections explicitly (FAQ, comparison table, guarantee) and dismantle each one.

## B10 - SEO over humans
**Detect:** Keyword-stuffed sentences that read unnaturally.
**Fix:** Restore the natural, empathetic voice; keywords only where they read as human language.
