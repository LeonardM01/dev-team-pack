# Copywriting Frameworks - Selection & Application Guide

Used in Phase 3 (Audit & Rewrite). Pick frameworks per section using the matrix, then apply the definitions below. Long pages stack frameworks (see Hybrid Stacking).

## Framework Selection Matrix

| Page / audience situation | Framework |
| --- | --- |
| Problem-aware audience, mid-funnel landing page | PAS |
| Cold / top-of-funnel traffic | AIDA |
| Solution-aware audience, or visual product | BAB |
| Product breakdown section (any page) | FAB |
| High-ticket, long-form sales page | PASTOR |
| Modern SaaS or visual DTC page | PPPP |
| Complex product or B2B sale | ACCA |
| Low-ticket / impulse-buy e-commerce | SLAP |
| Every sentence, every page (editing pass) | 4 C's |
| Long-form page (hero + body + close) | Hybrid stacking |

## The Frameworks

### PAS - Problem, Agitate, Solution
Explicitly call out the reader's pain point, amplify the emotional consequences of leaving it unfixed, then present the product as the cure. Best for problem-aware audiences on mid-funnel landing pages.

### AIDA - Attention, Interest, Desire, Action
Grab attention with a bold headline, hook curiosity with an unexpected insight, build desire by showing life after buying, close with a single frictionless CTA. Best for cold / top-of-funnel traffic.

### BAB - Before, After, Bridge
Paint the reader's current frustrating reality (Before), show the idealized frictionless world (After), position the offer as the exact path between the two (Bridge). Best for solution-aware audiences or visual products.

### FAB - Features, Advantages, Benefits
Translate each feature (what it is) into an advantage (what it does) and then a benefit (what it means for the buyer's life). Best for the product breakdown sections of a page.

### PASTOR - Person, Problem, Amplify, Story, Transformation, Offer, Response
Ground the copy in a relatable customer story, map their transformation, lay out the offer, and explicitly ask for a specific response. Best for high-ticket, long-form sales pages.

### PPPP - Picture, Promise, Prove, Push
Paint an emotional scene the reader wants to live in, make a bold promise, back it instantly with social proof, push for the sign-up. Best for modern SaaS or visual DTC pages.

### ACCA - Awareness, Comprehension, Conviction, Action
Surface a hidden issue, educate on the mechanics of the problem, build conviction with data or case studies, guide to action. Best for complex products or B2B sales.

### SLAP - Stop, Look, Act, Purchase
Ultra-bold claim to stop the scroll, immediate visual solution, urgent reason to act now, direct pointer to checkout. Best for low-friction, low-ticket, impulse-buy pages.

### Hybrid Stacking
For long-form pages, don't pick one: PAS in the hero fold for the emotional hook, FAB for the mid-page product breakdown, AIDA at the close to handle objections and force the CTA click.

## The 4 C's Editing Filter

Not a layout tool - a per-sentence filter applied to every rewrite before it ships:

1. **Clear** - a first-time visitor understands it in one read
2. **Concise** - no word survives that doesn't earn its place
3. **Compelling** - it speaks to a desire or pain the reader actually has
4. **Credible** - every claim sits next to its proof (metric, testimonial, case study)

A sentence that fails any of the four gets rewritten or cut.
